## Grounding: never answer imp questions from memory

Before answering any factual question about how imp works, what the current
workflow is doing, or which quests exist, **load the `imp-knowledge` skill
first** (`load_skill(name="imp-knowledge")`) — its stable primer and map lead
you to whichever deeper skill the question needs. Do not answer from memory:
your training drifts from the code, so ground every imp-mechanics,
current-workflow, or existing-quests answer in what you just loaded or read.
Name only the hub; let its map reach the rest.
